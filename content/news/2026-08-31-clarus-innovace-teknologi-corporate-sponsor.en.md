# PT Clarus Innovace Teknologi Joins BlankOn Foundation as a Corporate Sponsor

---

<img src="content/assets/clarus.svg" alt="PT Clarus Innovace Teknologi" width="320" />

BlankOn Foundation is pleased to announce PT Clarus Innovace Teknologi as its newest corporate sponsor. The support comes in the form of a virtual private server (VPS) that will help sustain the foundation's infrastructure needs and the projects it hosts.

## Infrastructure Support

The virtual machine sponsored by PT Clarus Innovace Teknologi comes with the following specifications:

- **Processor**: 8 cores
- **Memory**: 16 GB RAM
- **Storage**: 2 TB

Infrastructure is one of the most fundamental and most expensive needs of any open source project. Support like this allows the foundation to direct its resources toward project development, educational programs, and strengthening the community.

## About PT Clarus Innovace Teknologi

PT Clarus Innovace Teknologi is a Jakarta-based system integrator and IT services company, operating under the tagline *Your Trusted ICT Business Partner*. The company offers a range of solutions spanning data center and cloud infrastructure, enterprise collaboration, digital business platforms, managed services, security operations center, network and IT security, and big data and analytics.

PT Clarus Innovace Teknologi website: [https://clarus-it.co.id/](https://clarus-it.co.id/)

BlankOn Foundation extends its gratitude to PT Clarus Innovace Teknologi for its trust and its support of the open source movement in Indonesia.
