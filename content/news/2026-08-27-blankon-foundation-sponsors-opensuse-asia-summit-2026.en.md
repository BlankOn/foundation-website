# BlankOn Foundation Supports openSUSE.Asia Summit 2026 in Yogyakarta as a Custom Sponsor

---

![openSUSE.Asia Summit 2026 banner, October 03 - 04, 2026, Convention Hall at UIN Sunan Kalijaga, Yogyakarta](/content/assets/osas2026yogyakarta.png)

BlankOn Foundation is proud to announce its support for openSUSE.Asia Summit 2026 as a Custom Sponsor. The summit will be held on October 3–4, 2026, at the Convention Hall of UIN Sunan Kalijaga in the Special Region of Yogyakarta, Indonesia. This support is part of BlankOn Foundation's commitment to strengthening Indonesia's open source ecosystem, not only through the projects it develops, but also by backing community events that bring contributors and users together.

The summit is one of Asia's largest gatherings of the openSUSE community, and this year Indonesia has the opportunity to host it.

BlankOn Foundation believes that an event of this scale brings tangible benefits to the Indonesian community, from knowledge exchange with contributors across Asia, to wider opportunities for local developers, students, and communities to get involved in global open source projects.

## About openSUSE.Asia Summit 2026

openSUSE.Asia Summit is one of the flagship events for the openSUSE community in Asia, bringing together both contributors and users. People who normally interact only online can meet face to face, share their latest knowledge and experience, and learn about the FLOSS technologies surrounding openSUSE.

- **Date**: October 3–4, 2026
- **Venue**: Convention Hall at UIN Sunan Kalijaga, Jl. Laksda Adisucipto, Papringan, Caturtunggal, Kec. Depok, Kabupaten Sleman, Special Region of Yogyakarta, Indonesia
- **Event page**: [https://events.opensuse.org/conferences/oSAS26](https://events.opensuse.org/conferences/oSAS26)

## Join the Summit

The summit is open to anyone with an interest in open source, from long-time contributors to those who are just getting started.

- **Tickets**: [https://www.loket.com/event/osas26](https://www.loket.com/event/osas26)
- **Full schedule**: [https://events.opensuse.org/conferences/oSAS26/schedule](https://events.opensuse.org/conferences/oSAS26/schedule)

BlankOn Foundation invites the Indonesian open source community to take part in openSUSE.Asia Summit 2026 and to meet fellow contributors from across Asia in Yogyakarta.

## How You Can Support

If you would like to support the summit as well, there are three ways to do it.

1. **Sponsor the summit directly.** Organizations that want to back the event can arrange sponsorship with the openSUSE.Asia Summit 2026 committee at [sponsor@opensuse.id](mailto:sponsor@opensuse.id?subject=oSAS26%20Sponsorship).
2. **Become an individual sponsor by buying a Tiket Dukungan.** Alongside the regular and student tickets, the committee offers a support ticket called *Tiket Dukungan*, available at Rp500,000 and Rp1,000,000 for individuals who want to contribute beyond the price of admission. It can be purchased through [loket.com](https://www.loket.com/event/osas26).
3. **Donate to BlankOn Foundation.** For support with a broader scope, your donation helps us back the open source movement in Indonesia beyond any single event. [Donate to BlankOn Foundation](https://blankon.id/en/donate).

Every contribution, big or small, helps sustain Indonesia's open source ecosystem and the communities building it.
