# BlankOn Foundation Provides Infrastructure Support for IGOS Nusantara

---

BlankOn Foundation announces its infrastructure support for IGOS Nusantara, a local Linux distribution that has long been part of the story of Indonesia's open source movement. This support reflects BlankOn Foundation's ongoing commitment to strengthening Indonesia's open source ecosystem, not only through the development of BlankOn Linux, but also by embracing and assisting other open source projects that share the same vision of technological independence.

## About IGOS Nusantara

<img src="content/assets/igos-nusantara.jpeg" alt="IGOS Nusantara" />

IGOS Nusantara is a Fedora-based Linux distribution born from the Indonesia, Go Open Source! (IGOS) movement. The project was developed to encourage the adoption of open source software across Indonesia, in government, education, and the wider public, by providing an operating system that is easy to use and tailored to the needs of Indonesian users.

BlankOn Foundation hopes this infrastructure support will help the IGOS Nusantara team continue developing the project sustainably, enabling more people across Indonesia to use open source software in their daily lives.

BlankOn Foundation invites the wider community to support and learn more about local open source projects like IGOS Nusantara, as part of a shared effort to build technological independence in Indonesia.

IGOS Nusantara website: [https://igos-nusantara.or.id/](https://igos-nusantara.or.id/)

## Caring for the Legacy of Indonesia's Open Source Movement

BlankOn Linux and IGOS Nusantara grew from the same spirit: delivering an open source operating system built by and for the people of Indonesia. In line with BlankOn Foundation's focus on supporting and strengthening the broader ecosystem, community, projects, and activities related to open source and technological independence, providing infrastructure support to IGOS Nusantara is a concrete step toward making sure local projects can keep running and growing. BlankOn Linux and IGOS Nusantara used to compete with each other. We will keep competing, but now we compete to bring the very best to our users, while supporting each other so that Indonesia's open source movement grows ever stronger.

## How to Support Us

There are several ways you can support this collaboration between BlankOn Foundation and IGOS Nusantara:

- **Donate to BlankOn Foundation**: your contribution helps fund infrastructure support for IGOS Nusantara and other open source projects across Indonesia. [Donate to BlankOn Foundation](https://blankon.id/en/donate).
- **Use and contribute to IGOS Nusantara**: try IGOS Nusantara, report the issues you find, and help spread the word about the project. [Visit IGOS Nusantara](https://igos-nusantara.or.id/).

Every contribution, big or small, helps sustain Indonesia's open source ecosystem and the communities building it.
