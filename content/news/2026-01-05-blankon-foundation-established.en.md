# BlankOn Foundation Officially Established

---

# A New Chapter Begins

## Continuing the legacy of YPLI

The BlankOn Foundation has been officially established to continue the legacy of Yayasan Penggerak Linux Indonesia (YPLI) in advancing the open source technology ecosystem and technological independence in Indonesia.

## Looking ahead

The Foundation will focus on maintaining BlankOn Linux, promoting open source technology education, and strengthening the open source community throughout Indonesia.
