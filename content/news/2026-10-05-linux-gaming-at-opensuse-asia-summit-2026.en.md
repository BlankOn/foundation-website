# Linux Gaming Experience at openSUSE Asia Summit 2026

---

On Saturday and Sunday, October 3-4, 2026, openSUSE Asia Summit 2026 was successfully held at the Convention Hall of UIN Sunan Kalijaga. openSUSE Asia Summit is one of the major events for the openSUSE community, both contributors and users, in Asia. Members of the community who usually communicate online can get together from all over the world, talk face to face, and have fun. They share their most recent knowledge and experiences, and learn about FLOSS (Free and Libre Open Source Software) technologies surrounding openSUSE.

Muhammad Taufiq Nuruzzaman, Ph.D. of UIN Sunan Kalijaga opened the first day with the opening keynote. Patrick Fitzgerald, founder of the Geeko Foundation and an openSUSE member, continued the program with the second keynote, titled "The Great Migration".

The second day was just as interesting, with a keynote from Sunny SUSE on leadership in the open source community ("Leadership in the Open Source Community"). Afterwards, Douglas DeMaio of openSUSE took the stage to share the latest updates on the Geeko Foundation.

## Linux Gaming Indonesia

Outside the keynote hall and classrooms, BlankOn Foundation and GNOME Indonesia livened up the event by bringing Linux Gaming Indonesia. At the Linux Gaming Indonesia booth, visitors could try out gaming gear for free. Running Bazzite, the booth offered a game console experience, a portable game console, sim racing, and VR gaming that all run seamlessly on Linux.

![Visitors crowding the Linux Gaming Indonesia booth at openSUSE Asia Summit 2026](/content/assets/osas26-linux-gaming-1.jpeg)

![Portable game consoles and keyboards at the Linux Gaming Indonesia booth](/content/assets/osas26-linux-gaming-2.jpeg)

The game console ran on a mini PC powered by an Intel Core i7 9700, an AMD RX 7400 6GB GPU, and 16GB of DDR4 RAM, running the Bazzite AMD Home Theater PC edition with the GNOME desktop environment. Games such as It Takes Two, Stray, Dirt, FIFA 22, and more ran well with two Xbox controllers. The console became the best spot at the event for a relaxed session for two.

![Visitors playing FIFA 22 on the Bazzite game console](/content/assets/osas26-linux-gaming-4.jpeg)

![A visitor playing with a controller in front of the Linux Gaming Indonesia banner](/content/assets/osas26-linux-gaming-3.jpeg)

Beat Saber and Half-Life: Alyx could be played with a Pico VR headset connected to a computer also running Bazzite. Besides the console, Dirt could also be played on another computer fitted with sim racing controllers, running the same Bazzite operating system.

![A visitor trying VR gaming in the VR Zone](/content/assets/osas26-linux-gaming-5.jpeg)

![A visitor trying sim racing at the Linux Gaming Indonesia booth](/content/assets/osas26-linux-gaming-6.jpeg)

Two ThinkPad laptops, one of them with a touchscreen, were installed with BlankOn Linux, release name Sinambung, so visitors could try BlankOn Linux on real laptops and a touchscreen device. Bootable flash drives were also available to borrow for installing BlankOn Linux on the spot.

![A visitor trying BlankOn Linux Sinambung on a touchscreen ThinkPad](/content/assets/osas26-blankon-linux.jpg)

Besides Linux Gaming Indonesia, the booth area was also filled by openSUSE, Novacloud, Sepatu Fans Indonesia, Belajar FreeBSD, and a children's play area with building blocks.

Many booths handed out freebies in the form of stickers. The openSUSE booth, besides giving away free stickers, also ran a quiz with attractive merchandise prizes, while Novacloud held a mini game for booth visitors.

![Visitors gathered around the BlankOn booth table with laptops and stickers](/content/assets/osas26-linux-gaming-7.jpeg)

Thank you to the openSUSE Asia Summit 2026 organizers, UIN Sunan Kalijaga, GNOME Indonesia, and all the visitors who stopped by the Linux Gaming Indonesia booth. See you at the next event.
