# BlankOn Foundation Booth at Kubernetes Community Days Indonesia 2026 in Bandung

---

![Panel discussion "AI and Us: Who Replaces Whom?" at KCD Indonesia 2026](/content/assets/kcd26-stage.jpeg)

On Saturday, October 24, 2026, Kubernetes Community Days (KCD) Indonesia 2026 was held at Selah Hall, Piset Square, Bandung. KCD Indonesia is a community-organized conference under the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) that brings together adopters and technologists from the open source and cloud native communities to learn, collaborate, and network. This year's edition was a full-day event with more than 350 attendees, around 30 speakers, and over 27 sessions across four tracks. It continues the KCD Indonesia events held in 2021, 2022, and 2024.

BlankOn Foundation took part by opening a booth in the exhibition area, alongside community partners and corporate sponsors.

![The BlankOn Foundation booth at KCD Indonesia 2026, with BlankOn Linux Sinambung running on a touchscreen laptop](/content/assets/kcd26_photo_6260136678030774185_w.jpg)

## Try BlankOn Linux Sinambung

The main attraction of the booth was BlankOn Linux 26.09, release name Sinambung. A ThinkPad with a touchscreen, set up in tent mode, and a second laptop were ready for visitors, with a simple invitation on the table: "BlankOn Linux Sinambung. Please give it a try and share your feedback!"

![Visitors trying BlankOn Linux Sinambung on a touchscreen laptop](/content/assets/kcd26_photo_6260136678030774224_w.jpg)

Many attendees stopped by between sessions to explore the desktop, ask questions about BlankOn Linux, and talk about open source in Indonesia. We received a great deal of valuable feedback from visitors, ranging from practical suggestions for the next releases to ideas that will shape BlankOn Linux's long-term roadmap.

![BlankOn Foundation team chatting with visitors at the booth](/content/assets/kcd26_photo_6260136678030774226_w.jpg)

The booth also offered BlankOn flyers on building Indonesia's digital sovereignty, stickers from BlankOn and other open source projects, and information on how to support the foundation's work.

![Visitors exploring BlankOn Linux next to the "Support BlankOn" information stand](/content/assets/kcd26_photo_6260136678030774273_w.jpg)

Thank you to the KCD Indonesia 2026 organizers, Cloud Native Indonesia, and Google Developer Group (GDG) Bandung for providing space for our booth. It really means a lot to us. Thank you as well to everyone who visited the BlankOn Foundation booth. See you at the next event.
