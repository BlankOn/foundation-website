# Debian Day 2026 Purbalingga: BlankOn Foundation Discusses What It Means to Be a Derivative Project

---

![Debian Day 2026 Purbalingga poster](content/assets/debian-day-2026-purbalingga-poster.jpg)

Debian Day 2026 Purbalingga was held on Saturday, August 22, 2026, from 08:00 to 12:00 WIB, at Room C101, Building C, 1st Floor, Faculty of Engineering, Universitas Jenderal Soedirman (Unsoed), in a hybrid format that allowed participants to join both in person and online. The event was part of the CIErS Days 2026 series, "Connecting Researchers, Empowering Engineers," organized by the Electrical Engineering Department and the KBK TKI of Unsoed's Electrical Engineering Department.

## Three topics on Debian and free software

The event covered three main topics in the open source world: Debian & Free Software, Infrastructure & Open Applications, and a dedicated session titled Free/Open Source Development: What Does It Mean to Be a Derivative Project?, presented by Herpiko Dwi Aguno from BlankOn Foundation.

## BlankOn Foundation: what does it mean to be a derivative project?

In his session, Herpiko Dwi Aguno explored what it means to be a derivative project within the open source ecosystem, drawing on BlankOn's own experience as a Linux distribution built on the Debian base. The talk covered the responsibilities, challenges, and opportunities that derivative projects face in maintaining a healthy relationship with their upstream project, while still building their own identity and added value.

The session served as an important moment to further introduce BlankOn Foundation's role in Indonesia's open source ecosystem to the participants of Debian Day 2026 Purbalingga, particularly academics and students from Unsoed's Electrical Engineering Department.
