# BlankOn Foundation Officially Established

---

## Continuing the legacy of YPLI

The BlankOn Foundation has been officially established to continue the legacy of Yayasan Penggerak Linux Indonesia (YPLI) in advancing the open source technology ecosystem and technological independence in Indonesia.

## Looking ahead

The BlankOn Foundation marks a new dawn for Indonesia's open source ecosystem.

BlankOn is no longer just about a Linux distribution — it's leveling up. This new foundation will work to support and strengthen the ecosystem, community, projects, and activities related to open source technology and technological independence.

BlankOn Linux is also leveling up, striving to become a better, more inclusive, stable, and sustainable Linux distribution.

Long live independence!
