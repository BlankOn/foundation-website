BlankOn Foundation is pleased to announce 8Labs as its newest corporate sponsor. The support comes in the form of an AMD64 server that will help sustain the foundation's infrastructure needs and the projects it hosts.

The machine was previously sponsored personally by Mohammad Raska (Adekabang) as an individual sponsor. From now on, that support continues and is formalized through 8Labs as a corporate entity.

## Infrastructure Support

The server sponsored by 8Labs comes with the following specifications:

- **Architecture**: AMD64
- **Processor**: 56 cores
- **Memory**: 500 GB RAM
- **Storage**: 4.3 TB

Infrastructure is one of the most fundamental and most expensive needs of any open source project. Capacity at this scale gives the foundation ample room for compute-heavy work such as package building, continuous integration, and testing, allowing its resources to be directed toward project development, educational programs, and strengthening the community.

## About 8Labs

8Labs is an Indonesia-based provider of virtual and cloud lab environments, built around a simple idea: give people a safe place to experiment and fail in a controlled way, long before anything reaches production. Its offerings include Virtual Labs, Virtual Dedicated Labs, CloudLabs, and ElasticLabs, with support for a range of Linux and BSD distributions and configurable IPv6, IPv4, or NAT networking.

Beyond lab services, 8Labs also offers custom web application and SaaS development, setup and hardening of self-hosted open source solutions such as Nextcloud, git hosting, and team chat systems, as well as server monitoring, security, and maintenance services.

8Labs website: [https://8labs.id/](https://8labs.id/)

BlankOn Foundation extends its gratitude to 8Labs for its trust and its support of the open source movement in Indonesia. Our particular thanks go to Mohammad Raska (Adekabang), who supported the foundation personally from the start and has now bridged this sponsorship.
