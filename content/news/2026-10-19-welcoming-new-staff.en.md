# Welcoming Three New Staff Members to BlankOn Foundation

---

BlankOn Foundation is delighted to welcome three new people who have joined the foundation's staff: Riza Disti, Sistiandy Syahbana Nugraha, and Siska Iskandar.

As the programs, partners, and communities we support continue to grow, the foundation needs a team that can manage day-to-day work in a more structured way. Their arrival complements the foundation's leadership and strengthens our ability to carry out our mission of advancing open source technology in Indonesia.

## Riza Disti, Finance Manager

<img src="content/assets/riza-finance-manager.png" alt="Riza Disti" class="team-photo" />

Riza Disti joins as Finance Manager. During her professional career on a large-scale project, Riza worked in project report administration and helped the HR department calculate project team expenses, process payroll and deductions, and verify data, while also organizing events. This hands-on experience with expense tracking, payroll, and data verification is valuable for us, as it provides a solid foundation for managing the foundation's finances carefully and transparently.

In this new role, Riza is responsible for managing the foundation's finances, from recording donations and sponsorships and managing program budgets to preparing financial reports.

As a non-profit funded entirely by donations, sponsorships, and grants, transparency matters deeply to us. Riza will make sure that every contribution we receive is properly recorded and used for its intended purpose.

## Sistiandy Syahbana Nugraha, Public Relations

<img src="content/assets/sis-public-relation.jpeg" alt="Sistiandy Syahbana Nugraha" class="team-photo" />

Sistiandy Syahbana Nugraha joins as Public Relations staff. Sistiandy is a long-standing volunteer with Relawan TIK, Indonesia's ICT volunteer movement, and was part of the BlankOn Linux project's public relations team before turning his focus to the foundation.

In this new role, Sistiandy will act as the bridge between the foundation and the public, the media, communities, and partners, sharing news about the foundation's work and building new collaborations.

For media, partnership, or sponsorship inquiries, you can reach us at [humas@blankon.id](mailto:humas@blankon.id).

## Siska Iskandar, GEDSI Coordinator

<img src="content/assets/siska-community-manager.png" alt="Siska Iskandar" class="team-photo" />

Siska Iskandar joins as GEDSI (Gender Equality, Disability, and Social Inclusion) Coordinator. Siska is an activist in Indonesia's open source movement who has been involved in major events held in Indonesia, such as GNOME Asia Summit and openSUSE Asia Summit. Siska is also an openSUSE member and currently serves on the openSUSE Marketing Team.

In this new role, Siska will make sure the foundation's programs and activities are open and accessible to everyone, regardless of gender, disability, or social background.

Open source ecosystems thrive when everyone feels welcome to learn and contribute. This role reaffirms the foundation's commitment to building a more inclusive technology community in Indonesia.

## Changes in Leadership Roles

Alongside welcoming our new staff, we have also adjusted roles within the foundation's leadership. Akhmat Safrudin now serves as Program Manager, while Iwan stwn moves into the role of Supervisor.

Akhmat brings more than a decade of experience in managing non-profit organizations, including AirPutih. That background in NGO work makes him well suited to coordinating the foundation's increasingly diverse programs, from running events to supporting communities and partner initiatives.

Iwan will continue to serve the foundation as Supervisor and advisor. In this role, Iwan will help us maintain prudence in every decision and step the foundation takes.

## A Step Toward Equality

Riza and Siska joining the team matters to us in our pursuit of equality. With them on board, women now make up 28% of the foundation's team. That is not yet enough, and we are aiming for at least 40%.

We believe a diverse team makes better decisions and builds programs that are more relevant to every part of society. We will keep this commitment in mind as we recruit and grow the team in the future.

## Welcome Aboard

We extend a warm welcome to Riza, Sistiandy, and Siska, and we look forward to their contributions to the foundation and the communities we support.
