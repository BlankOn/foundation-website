# Arfhacom Joins BlankOn Foundation as a Corporate Sponsor

---

<img src="content/assets/arfhacom-sponsor.jpeg" alt="Arfhacom" width="320" />

BlankOn Foundation is pleased to announce Arfhacom as its newest corporate sponsor. Arfhacom will support the foundation through recurring monthly donations ranging from IDR 500,000 to IDR 1,000,000 each month.

## It Started with Devices for Contributors

This is not the first time Arfhacom has supported us. Earlier, Arfhacom donated IDR 8,000,000, which the foundation used to purchase computing devices for its contributors. Adequate hardware is essential for contributors to develop, test, and maintain the open source projects the foundation hosts.

Arfhacom has now decided to continue its support on an ongoing basis as a sponsor. Recurring donations like this mean a great deal to the foundation, as predictable funding helps us plan our programs and cover operating costs with more confidence.

## About Arfhacom

Arfhacom is an independent information technology contractor based in Bogor, West Java. It was founded by a seasoned engineer who has worked in both local and international environments since 2010. Its services span IT helpdesk and support, networks and infrastructure, system and cloud administration, cybersecurity, IT training, and IT consulting, with a focus on small, medium, and mid-market organizations.

Arfhacom puts open source solutions first in its recommendations, while tailoring them to each client's needs.

Arfhacom website: [https://arfha.com/](https://arfha.com/)

BlankOn Foundation extends its gratitude to Arfhacom for its trust and its commitment to the open source movement in Indonesia.

## How to Support Us

If you or your organization would like to support technological independence in Indonesia, there are several ways to help:

- **Become a sponsor.** We welcome support in the form of funding, infrastructure, or services. Reach us at [humas@blankon.id](mailto:humas@blankon.id) or visit the [sponsorship page](https://blankon.id/en/sponsorship).
- **Donate.** We accept one-time and recurring monthly donations. [Donate to BlankOn Foundation](https://blankon.id/en/donate).

Every contribution, big or small, helps sustain Indonesia's open source ecosystem and the communities building it.
