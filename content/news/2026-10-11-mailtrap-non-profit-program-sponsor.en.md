# BlankOn Foundation Joins Mailtrap's Non-Profit Program

---

<img src="content/assets/mailtrap-light.svg" alt="Mailtrap" width="320" class="block dark:hidden" />
<img src="content/assets/mailtrap-dark.svg" alt="Mailtrap" width="320" class="hidden dark:block" />

BlankOn Foundation is pleased to announce that its application to the [Mailtrap](https://mailtrap.io/) non-profit program has been accepted, making Mailtrap the foundation's newest sponsor. The program gives qualifying non-profit organizations access to its email platform at no cost.

## Service Support

Mailtrap is an email delivery platform that offers an Email API and SMTP service for sending transactional and bulk email, as well as an Email Sandbox for inspecting and debugging email in development, staging, and QA environments.

Through this program, the foundation gains access to Mailtrap's Team plan. This helps us test and deliver email reliably for the foundation's services and the projects it hosts, without adding to the running costs we cover from donations.

## About Mailtrap

Mailtrap is an email delivery platform built to help product teams ensure fast email delivery and high inboxing rates. Beyond its commercial offering, Mailtrap runs a program that makes its tooling available to non-profit organizations and open source projects.

Mailtrap website: [https://mailtrap.io/](https://mailtrap.io/)

BlankOn Foundation extends its gratitude to Mailtrap for accepting our application and for its support of the open source movement in Indonesia.

## How to Support Us

If you or your organization would like to support technological independence in Indonesia, there are several ways to help:

- **Become a sponsor.** We welcome support in the form of funding, infrastructure, or services. Reach us at [humas@blankon.id](mailto:humas@blankon.id) or visit the [sponsorship page](https://blankon.id/en/sponsorship).
- **Donate.** We accept one-time and recurring monthly donations. [Donate to BlankOn Foundation](https://blankon.id/en/donate).

Every contribution, big or small, helps sustain Indonesia's open source ecosystem and the communities building it.
