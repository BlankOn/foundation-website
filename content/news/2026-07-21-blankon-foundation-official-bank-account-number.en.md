# BlankOn Foundation's Official Bank Account

---

BlankOn Foundation is pleased to announce the establishment of an official corporate bank account, following the completion of the foundation's administrative and legal processes. This milestone marks an important step toward building a transparent and accountable financial foundation to support the sustainability of the open source movement and technological independence in Indonesia.

Previously, donation funds were managed through a contributor's personal bank account, a practice that carried inherent governance and accountability risks. With the establishment of this official account, all funds received and managed will be overseen jointly by the BlankOn Foundation Team, ensuring greater accountability to the public and to our donors.

Going forward, all donations to BlankOn Foundation should be directed to the following official account:

- Bank: Bank BNI
- Account Number: 6111622231
- Account Name: BlankOn Penggiat Sumber Terbuka Yayasan

BlankOn Foundation extends its gratitude for the continued trust and support from the community and donors in advancing the open source ecosystem in Indonesia.
