# JKTLUG September Meetup

---

The Jakarta Linux Users Group ([jktlug.id](https://jktlug.id)) held a meetup on Sunday, September 27, 2026, from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM WIB at the Ruang Berkarya, HB Jassin Jakarta Library. JKTLUG initiated the gathering with support from BlankOn Foundation, GNOME Indonesia, Dispusip, and Perpustakaan Jakarta.

The meetup featured three main speakers across two sharing sessions. Rin took a deep dive into Nix, while Andika Triwidada and Rania Amina discussed language localization.

## From Nix Packages to Language Localization

Adi Ginting introduced the Jakarta Linux Users Group. JKTLUG, which was only declared last month, is expected to bring fresh energy and fill the gap left in Jakarta's Linux community. It is an effort to create a space that is easy for newcomers to enter, while building a culture that actively encourages members to contribute to upstream projects and distributions, and to engage with regional Linux user groups.

![Adi Ginting introducing the Jakarta Linux Users Group](/content/assets/jktlug-meetup-27-september-2026-adi.jpeg)

The sharing sessions began with Rin, who shared their experience with Nix in a talk titled "NIX 107: Love, Logic, and a Certain Past". It was a thorough walkthrough of Nix and how it works. The session ended with a Q&A and discussion with attendees, ranging from technical details of Nix to the behavior of the very active Nix community.

![Rin presenting "NIX 107: Love, Logic, and a Certain Past"](/content/assets/jktlug-meetup-27-september-2026-rin.jpeg)

The second session was delivered by Andika Triwidada, a translation coordinator, together with Rania Amina of Nenggala Kreatif ID, who is also an Official Member of TDF (LibreOffice), on the topic "Translating GNOME". The two shared their motivations for translating open source projects: a mix of personal challenge, the desire for digital sovereignty, and a form of inclusivity in open digital technology.

![Andika Triwidada presenting "Translating GNOME"](/content/assets/jktlug-meetup-27-september-2026-andika-triwidada.jpeg)

Andika Triwidada also highlighted the importance of generational renewal in the project. Andika also covered what a translator does, how to become a contributor, and the benefits of being an active contributor. In this session, Rania Amina also demonstrated a tool built on local LLM technology that helps make repetitive translation work more efficient. The session ended with a Q&A.

The event closed with a networking session, where attendees could get to know each other and their backgrounds, and discuss topics that did not fit into the earlier Q&A. As a finishing touch, attendees received freebies in the form of GNOME, BlankOn, JKTLUG, and Debian stickers.
